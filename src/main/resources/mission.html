How to use this site

01

Pick a topic

Choose what aspect of road safety you want to explore — injuries, vehicle types, or dangerous combinations involving people.

02

Filter the data

Use the dropdowns and checkboxes to narrow the data down to the vehicle type, injury outcome, age group, or year range that matters to you.

03

Discover patterns

The Deep Dive page ranks dangerous combinations by how far above average their fatal rate is — surfacing facts that basic summaries would hide.
